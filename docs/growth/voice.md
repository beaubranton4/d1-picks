# Voice guide (growth loop)

Nerdy and analytical, calm about the math, plain in delivery. Short sentences. Lead with how the rule works, then an example of what that means for a team, then what the NCAA's page does not say. Write as a reference: no "I" or "we tested", no first-hand claims, no "watched" or "saw".

- Explain how seeding, the RPI, selection and ratings work, using only the cited NCAA and governing-body pages for rules, thresholds, dates and counts.
- Our own ratings and results are linked, not quoted: do not state any rating, probability, win chance, record or team ranking of ours. The page template shows live numbers beside the guide.
- Never say who will win, which team to back, who is "due", or what any team's chances are in a game. No predictions, projections, picks, bets, odds, lines, "edges" or "value". If a sentence could be read as advice to bet, delete it.
- Do not name sportsbooks, prediction markets or promotions. Do not mention profit, bankroll or units.
- Women's D1 volleyball only. Do not write about other sports or levels.
- No hype, no superlatives, no "lock", no "guaranteed". Examples with made-up numbers must start "Example:" and carry no link.
