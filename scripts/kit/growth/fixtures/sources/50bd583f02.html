Card policies

Cardholders may keep up to 50 items checked out at once. Most books circulate for 21 days and can be renewed 2 times if no one has a hold.

Digital loans of ebooks last 14 days. Overdue fines for physical items are $0.25 per day, capped at $5 per item.

Library staff process these requests at the circulation desk and by phone during regular branch hours, and every branch can answer questions about account standing, holds, and fines that may block a renewal.
