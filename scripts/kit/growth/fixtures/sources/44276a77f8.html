Renew your library card

Library cards expire after 3 years. You can renew online up to 30 days before the expiration date, or at any branch with a photo ID.

Renewal is free. A replacement for a lost card costs $2 at the circulation desk.

Renewal is blocked when an account owes more than $10 in fines. Pay the balance first, then renew.

Library staff process these requests at the circulation desk and by phone during regular branch hours, and every branch can answer questions about account standing, holds, and fines that may block a renewal.
